Settings for your keyboard and mouse switch.

iPhone or iPad: every browser there (Safari and Chrome too) lacks Web Bluetooth. Install the free Bluefy browser and open this page in it.
Android, Windows, Mac: use Chrome or Edge.
Settings not opened yet
Press Connect and pick “8REC-KM” in the list. This opens the hub’s settings. Your keyboard and mouse keep working either way.
Phone link is open
Anyone nearby can change settings until you close it. It closes by itself after a few minutes. To open it again later, hold Ctrl + Shift on the keyboard and type 8REC.

Keyboard and mouse

MX Keys (keyboard)-
MX Master (mouse)-
Put the MX device in pairing mode (hold its Easy-Switch button about 3 seconds). If the keyboard asks for a code, it shows here once pairing is open. The mouse needs no code.

Diagnostics

The hub keeps its last ~300 events (about half an hour of connections, drops and pairings) in memory. Read them here after something goes wrong. They are lost when the hub loses power.

Shortcuts

Ctrl + Shift + Left Arrow switches to computer 1, Ctrl + Shift + Right Arrow to computer 2.
Switch computer: hold Ctrl + Shift and click mouse button -
No-sleep on or off for the active computer: hold Ctrl + Shift and click mouse button -
No-sleep sends a tiny invisible key tap every 30 seconds so the computer never goes to sleep. On the hub, a red light stays on while computer 1 is selected and a blue light while computer 2 is selected. With no-sleep on, the selected computer's light dips off briefly every 2 seconds, and an unselected computer's light flashes on briefly. The green light shows pairing: it blinks fast while the hub is scanning, slowly while it waits for you to type a code, and is off when pairing is done.
